## 💡 About KhetiShare

KhetiShare is a digital platform designed to connect farmers with agricultural equipment owners. It helps farmers rent essential farming machinery at affordable prices instead of purchasing expensive equipment.

Our mission is to make farming more affordable, accessible, and technology-driven.

## 🚜 Key Features

- 🔍 Browse Agricultural Equipment
- 📍 Location-Based Equipment Search
- 💰 Affordable Rental Pricing
- 📅 Equipment Booking and Date Selection
- 👨‍🌾 Farmer and Equipment Owner Connection
- 💳 Demo Checkout Interface

## 🎯 Problem We Solve

Many small and marginal farmers cannot afford expensive agricultural machinery. At the same time, equipment owners often have machinery that remains unused.

KhetiShare aims to bridge this gap through a shared equipment rental platform.
